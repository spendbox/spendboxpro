# Newtown: the master plan

Newtown is a second life in the real world: the real Lagos first, then the whole planet, where
you can become who you want. Doctor, chef, footballer, space pilot, landlord, inventor. The world
changes over time, every place has its own culture, and no two days are the same.

This is the plan for everything. How the map itself is built is in `docs/WORLD.md`; what's
being worked on right now is in `TODO.md`.

---

## 1. The rules everything follows

1. **Like living life, without the chores.** Nobody brushes teeth. Routines do the everyday
   things; players spend their time on choices and big moments.
2. **Simple on the surface, deep underneath.** Five meters and a weekly routine are all a new
   player needs to understand. Depth comes from how things combine, not from more screens.
3. **No ceiling.** Skills, careers, fame, wealth and knowledge keep growing forever (slower the
   higher you go). There is always a next step.
4. **A few rules, endless stories.** Events come from rules combining with places, people and
   culture, never from a fixed list. New places get events with no new writing.
5. **One shared world.** Everyone in Lagos sees the same fire, the same new tower, the same
   news. What changes stays changed.
6. **Every place is itself.** Lagos feels like Lagos. Culture shapes names, food, music,
   festivals, customs and how things look.
7. **Fast on any phone.** Only what's near you is drawn and simulated in detail. Far away
   catches up in one quick sum when someone arrives.
8. **Safe and respectful.** 18+, consent built in, block and report everywhere, and no
   stereotypes in how real cultures are shown.

## 2. Time: how long is a day, a month, a year?

One clock for the whole world:

| In the game | Real time | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 day | 2 hours | Most visits see a sunrise or a sunset, and night life comes round often |
| 1 week (7 days) | 14 hours | A routine set in the morning has run its week by the next day |
| 1 month (30 days) | 2½ days | A pay cycle, a rent cycle, a season step |
| 1 year (12 months) | 30 days | **One real month is one game year** |

What that means in practice:

- **Routines** run every game day (12 times a real day), even while you're offline.
- **Building:** a house takes 3 to 7 game days (an evening to a night), a tower a few game
  months (about a real week), a stadium a game year.
- **Learning:** a course is a few game weeks; a degree 3 to 4 game years (3 to 4 real
  months); becoming a doctor about 6 game years (half a real year). That's a real goal, not a
  grind.
- **Trees** grow over game years and die of old age now and then; new ones get planted.
- **Seasons** follow each place's real seasons, sped up with the game year. Lagos has its
  rainy season (floods, traffic jams) and harmattan haze.
- **Big real festivals** (Christmas, Eid, Eyo, Detty December, New Year) also happen on their
  **real dates** as live, once-a-year events, so they stay special even though game years are
  short.
- **The sun** is in the right place for each part of the world (it's night in Tokyo while it's
  day in Lagos), on the game clock.
- **Ageing:** with a game year every real month, players would age fast. So players **choose**
  how their character ages: grow older normally, slow it down, or stay at an age they like.
  Healthy routines add years to their life. People you meet in town (NPCs) age and are
  replaced normally, so the city's population keeps changing.

The game counts years from its launch (Year 1, Year 2...), so history builds up: "the Third
Mainland Bridge rebuild of Year 3".

## 3. Your life

### The five meters

**Health, Energy, Mood, Money, Connections.** Everything feeds into these, and they feed into
everything (a happy, rested person works better; a sick one misses work).

### The routine

- A weekly plan made of blocks: **work, study, exercise, eat, socialise, rest, worship,
  hobby, side business...**
- It runs by itself in cycles, and you get better at each block the longer you keep it up.
- Add a block and feel the effect: exercise means better health, so fewer sick days and a
  longer life; eating well gives more energy and faster learning.
- New blocks unlock as you grow (an MBA block, a band practice block, a lab block).
- A suggested routine is built automatically when you start; change it any time.

### Moments

Short scenes where you actually play: a job interview, a first surgery, a pitch to investors, a
match, a first date, a wedding, a launch, a concert. They use the minigames the game already has
and decide how things go: the routine sets you up, the moment is where you shine.

### Skills with no ceiling

Every skill (cooking, medicine, flying, music, coding, building, speaking Yoruba...) levels up
forever. Level 100 isn't the end, just rare.

## 4. Things people can do (the big list)

### Body and mind
- **Eat:** meals keep energy up, good food gives bonuses, skipping meals slowly hurts. Real local
  dishes (jollof, suya, egusi, puff-puff, amala...), restaurants, street food, cooking at home.
- **Cook:** a skill; recipes from every culture; open a restaurant or a food stall.
- **Fitness:** gym, running, football, swimming, dance. Fitter players have more energy and are
  ill less often.
- **Health:** colds, injuries, check-ups, hospitals, doctors (players can be the doctors).
- **Rest and mood:** sleep, holidays, travel, hobbies, worship, therapy.
- **Ageing**, as you choose (see Time).

### Learning (infinite)
- Primary to university, courses, night school, online learning.
- Degrees and certificates that unlock careers (no medical degree, no surgery).
- Libraries, mentors, apprenticeships; **teaching** as a paid job.
- Languages from each culture: speak to more people, unlock more of a place.
- Research (see Discovery).

### Work and careers
- **Any career:** doctor, nurse, chef, lawyer, teacher, engineer, architect, pilot, astronaut,
  footballer, boxer, musician, DJ, actor, fashion designer, journalist, scientist, police
  officer, firefighter, farmer, banker, politician, game designer...
- **Career ladders:** study, then entry job, then promotions, then the top, each with a few big
  moments (a pilot's first solo flight, a lawyer's first case, a footballer's first final).
- **Jobs at any business** (already in the game: interview, shifts, pay), now kept for good.
- **Run a business:** shop, salon, restaurant, studio, clinic, school, bank, airline. Hire
  players and NPCs, set prices, grow it.
- **Fame:** music charts, box office, sports leagues and transfers, fashion weeks, awards nights.

### Money
- **Bank account, savings with interest, loans, mortgages** (see section 10, legal).
- **Investing:** shares in players' businesses, property.
- **Insurance:** matters once disasters can hit what you own.
- Pay, tips, rent, sales, prizes; taxes that fund the city.

### Property and building
- **Own anything:** a flat, a house, a shop, a tower, land. Every building has a fixed address
  now the world is permanent, so it can have an owner, a price and a history of sales.
- **Buy, sell, rent out,** with a full record of every owner.
- **Build a house:** buy a plot, pick a design (or design your own later), hire builders
  (players or NPCs), watch it go up over game days, then furnish it room by room (furniture from
  the marketplace).
- **Upgrade over time:** extensions, floors, a pool, a studio, a shop on the ground floor.
- **Disasters** (flood, fire, storm) can damage buildings; insurance and rebuilding follow.

### Making and trading
- **Craft:** clothes, furniture, art, food, music, vehicles, machines, from materials you buy
  or gather.
- **Marketplace:** sell what you make, set prices, take commissions, build a brand.
- **Your work lives in the world:** people wear your clothes, sit on your chairs, play your
  music in their clubs.

### Discovery and progress
- **Research:** scientists (players) put time and money into research projects.
- **Breakthroughs unlock things for everyone, worldwide:** electric cars, then solar roofs, then
  flying taxis, then space travel, then whatever the tree grows into. The world's technology
  moves forward because players moved it.
- **Credit forever:** each breakthrough names who discovered it.
- **Explore:** the first visitors to each new region as the map grows, and later space.

### People and love
- **Friends** (already in the game), best friends, rivals, mentors.
- **Dating:** meet, go on dates (restaurants, beaches, concerts), get closer over time.
- **Relationships:** together, engaged, married, with weddings in each culture's own style
  (a Lagos owambe wedding with aso ebi and spraying). Shared home, shared money if they choose.
- **Intimacy:** romantic and never explicit: dates, holding hands, a kiss, then fade to black.
  Both people must agree to every step, and either can stop it at any time.
- **Families** (later): children who grow up in game years, inheritance, family names that last.
- **Community:** clubs, churches and mosques, neighbourhood groups, charities, volunteering.
- **Civic life:** elections, becoming a councillor or the mayor, city budgets.

### Fun and nights out
- **Host a party:** own or rent a venue, invite people, pick the DJ (the jukebox already
  exists), set an entry fee, spray mint. Your party shows on the map as a live moment and in the
  news; good parties raise your fame.
- **Dance** (already in the game: moves, partners, lights on the beat).
- **Concerts, sports matches, cinema, beaches, festivals, tours** of famous places.
- **Minigames** (100 already built) everywhere they fit.
- **Rides:** trains, monorail, balloons, boats, helicopters (already in the game), and later
  your own car.

## 5. Talking: chat and voice

- **Text chat** (already in the game) in every place.
- **Voice:**
  - **Nearby voice:** hear people close to you, fading with distance, like a real street or
    party.
  - **Room voice:** everyone in the same place (a club, a meeting, a classroom).
  - **Calls:** to friends, on the game's phone (the phone already exists).
  - **Safety:** voice is off until you switch it on; mute and block in one tap; report; no
    recording; push-to-talk on phones. Voice needs a voice service (for example LiveKit), which
    costs money per minute used, so it starts in places like clubs and calls.

## 6. News

- **Made from what actually happened:** fires, openings, sales, matches, elections, discoveries,
  weddings of famous players, big parties. The facts come from the world; Claude writes the
  words (never inventing facts).
- **By place:** Lagos news in Lagos, local street news in your neighbourhood, world news for
  the big things.
- **Channels you subscribe to:** city news, sports, business, gossip, science, music.
- **Players can be journalists** and run their own channels; good channels get subscribers and
  pay.
- **A daily edition** each game day, plus breaking news for big events.
- **Style follows the culture** (a Lagos paper reads like a Lagos paper).

---

## 7. Lighting up (instead of ghosts)

- **Spotlight:** whoever did something remarkable today glows on the map. Tap them to see the
  story, cheer or send a gift.
- **Live moments:** someone's big moment (a final, a wedding, a concert, a launch, a party) lights
  up their spot, and anyone can come and watch.
- **Rising stars:** players climbing fastest glow brighter, with a weekly "who's rising" list.
- **Light up the city:** good deeds (charity, helping new players, rebuilding after a disaster)
  light up that neighbourhood at night.

## 8. A world that changes

Two layers (see `docs/WORLD.md`):

1. **The base world:** worked out from the map data and fixed rules. Costs nothing to store.
2. **The change layer:** a table of what actually changed (owners, damage, new buildings,
   history).

Most change is **calculated from the date**, not stored: trees grow and die, building sites
finish, buildings age and get repaired. Only what a rule can't predict is stored: player actions
and the results of disasters on things people own.

## 9. Events that never repeat

- **Things are made of parts.** A building is a set of parts (kitchen, stage, vault, seats,
  roof, owner, staff), and events attach to parts. Anything with a stage can host a concert.
- **People have traits, needs, money, jobs, relationships and memories.** Events come from
  them: a broke, greedy manager next to a vault is a possible inside job, without anyone writing
  "inside job at a Lekki bank".
- **Rules** say "if this is true, this can happen, and here's what changes". Each new rule
  combines with every part, person and place, so possibilities multiply.
- **Consequences stick:** a fire leaves a ruin, the ruin is rebuilt, the owner holds a grudge,
  the grudge sets up the next event.
- **A storyteller paces it:** how often, how big, where, so the world is never constant chaos
  or dead quiet.
- **Culture flavours it:** one "big celebration" rule is an owambe in Lagos and something else
  elsewhere.
- **Same for everyone:** events come from the world's rules and the time, so all players see
  the same thing; it's stored only once someone acts on it.
- **Claude for words only:** dialogue, headlines, descriptions. It never decides the rules.

## 10. Culture

A culture profile for each place, layered **world, continent, country, region, city,
neighbourhood**. Each layer only says what's different from the one above. It covers names,
languages and greetings, religions, food, music, transport, prices, working hours, markets, the
calendar (festivals and seasons), customs (owambe, weddings, naming ceremonies), building styles,
trees and street life.

People vary inside a culture: profiles set how common things are, never one trait for everyone.
Someone from each place reviews its profile before it goes live (Lagos: the owner).

## 12. The order to build it

**Stage 1: the world** (under way, see `docs/WORLD.md`)
1. Bake Lagos from the real map (running) and check it.
2. Every real named place in Lagos from the map data (churches, mosques, schools, markets...).
3. Smooth bridges (Third Mainland Bridge's curves), rail, beaches, Makoko, landmarks at real
   size; drawing in chunks.
4. Zoomed-out map; tap to fly anywhere.

**Stage 2: time and change**
5. The world clock (section 2): days, seasons, years, the sun in the right place.
6. Fixed addresses for every building, and the change layer.
7. Life over time: trees, construction, ageing buildings.

**Stage 3: your life**
8. The five meters and the routine (eat, sleep, exercise, work first).
9. Education and careers: full ladders for doctor, chef, musician and pilot first.
10. Bank account and savings.
11. Buying, selling and renting property; building a house.

**Stage 4: a living world**
12. The Lagos culture profile.
13. The event engine (parts, people, rules, storyteller), starting with about 30 rules.
14. News: made from events, by place, channels.
15. Spotlight and live moments.
16. Disasters and rebuilding; insurance.

**Stage 5: together**
17. Hosting parties.
18. Dating, relationships and weddings.
19. Voice: rooms and calls first, then nearby voice.
20. Crafting and the marketplace.

**Stage 6: the whole world**
21. The world bake on GitHub's automation, saved to Supabase Storage, each area loaded only when
    needed.
22. More cities (Abuja, Accra, Nairobi, London...), each with its culture profile.
23. Research and world-wide technology progress.
24. Families, civic life, space.

**Later (noted, not started):** 3D avatars for players and characters walking the world, and
the avatar studio for them.
