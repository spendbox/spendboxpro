# Leave your mark

Rules: `src/lib/world/marks.ts`. Game: `src/app/play/mark-actions.ts`, screens in
`src/app/play/life/marks-sheet.tsx`, drawn on the map by `city-view.tsx` (setMarkTags).
Database: `game-db/052_marks.sql`.

## What you can leave
| Mark | Costs | Stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing (any words, 8 inks) | free | until people vote it down, or you take it down |
| Review (1 to 5 stars and words) | free | the same |
| Tree | ₦20,000 | 30 days |
| Bench | ₦50,000 | 30 days |
| Plaque | ₦100,000 | 60 days |
| Statue | ₦500,000 | 30 days |
| Fountain | ₦2m | 30 days |
| Time capsule (a message, and one of your things inside) | ₦5,000 | opens on its date |

Leave one from your phone (Leave a mark: where the middle of your screen is) or from any building's
or place's card (Leave a mark here). Up to 20 a day.

## Free writing, and the players decide
- Anything can be written, and the author's name is always shown with it.
- The one thing the game blocks: real contact details (phone numbers, email addresses).
- Everyone can react (👍 😂 🔥 ❤️; the author is told) or vote to take it down. Five votes take it
  down (one more for every five reactions it has, so well-liked marks are harder to remove). The
  author loses 3 reputation and is told.
- Reactions people give to your marks add to your reputation (one point for every two, up to 50).

## Paid marks and capsules
- Anyone can chip in to keep a tree, statue... standing longer: a day costs a thirtieth of its
  price. The author is told who chipped in.
- A time capsule's message stays hidden until it opens. The first to open it after that gets the
  thing inside.
