# Leave your mark

Rules: `src/lib/world/marks.ts`. Game: `src/app/play/mark-actions.ts`, screens in
`src/app/play/life/marks-sheet.tsx`, drawn on the map by `city-view.tsx` (setMarkTags).
Database: `game-db/052_marks.sql`, then `053_marks_rules.sql` (styles, limits).

## What you can leave
| Mark | Costs | Stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing (a short line, 8 inks, 7 graffiti styles) | free | until people vote it down, or you take it down |
| Review (1 to 5 stars and words) | free | the same |
| Tree: plant a new one (₦20,000), or adopt one in a park or wood (₦10,000) | | for good |
| Bench | ₦50,000 | 30 days |
| Plaque | ₦100,000 | 60 days |
| Statue | ₦500,000 | 30 days |
| Fountain | ₦2m | 30 days |
| Time capsule (a message, and one of your things inside) | ₦5,000 | opens on its date |

Leave one from your phone (Leave a mark: where the middle of your screen is) or from any building's
or place's card (Leave a mark here). Up to 20 a day.

## Who can leave what, and how many
- You need **20 reputation** to leave anything.
- You can have **one mark up at a time, plus one more for every 50 reputation** (20-49: one;
  50-99: two; 100-149: three...). Take one down to make room.
- Writing is a short line: **100 letters** at most. Graffiti styles: spray, bubble, tag, drip,
  stencil, neon, chalk (each shown as it will look, before you leave it).

## Keeping the map tidy
- Nothing goes on water.
- Trees, benches, statues and fountains never stand on a road, a bridge or flyover, a railway, a
  station's site or a landmark: if the middle of your screen is on one, the mark goes on the
  nearest spot that will do (two tiles at most), or the game says there's no room.
- Trees can't go among office blocks and towers: plant them in gardens, parks and open ground.
  In a park or a wood, you adopt one of the trees already there.
- Writing, reviews, plaques and capsules are on walls and pavements, so they can go anywhere on land.
- On the map, marks are small tags over the spot, never new buildings, so roads and buildings
  stay exactly as they are.

## Free writing, and the players decide
- Anything can be written, and the author's name is always shown with it.
- What the game blocks: real contact details (phone numbers, email addresses), websites and links, and social media handles.
- Everyone can react (👍 😂 🔥 ❤️; the author is told) or vote to take it down. Five votes take it
  down (one more for every five reactions it has, so well-liked marks are harder to remove). The
  author loses 3 reputation and is told.
- Reactions people give to your marks add to your reputation (one point for every two, up to 50).

## Paid marks and capsules
- Anyone can chip in to keep a tree, statue... standing longer: a day costs a thirtieth of its
  price. The author is told who chipped in.
- A time capsule's message stays hidden until it opens. The first to open it after that gets the
  thing inside.
