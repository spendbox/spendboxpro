# Dating

Rules: `src/lib/world/street.ts` (DATE_ACTS). Game: `src/app/play/dating-actions.ts`. Screens:
`src/app/play/people/street-dating.tsx` (Dating on your phone, and the dating part of someone's
card). Database: `game-db/056_dating.sql` (tests: `game-db/tests/dating.test.sql`).

## Consent, always
- Newtown is for adults only (part 12). Dating is **opt-in**: switch on **Open to dating** in
  Dating on your phone. Only people who've switched it on can ask or be asked.
- **Nothing happens without a yes.** A date, a hug, a kiss, making out, and "make out ++" are each
  a question the other person answers with Yes or Not now. Every time.
- A "not now" can't be asked again for a day. One ask at a time; asks run out after a day.
- Either of you can **end it** any time; the other is told kindly. Switching dating off turns
  down everything waiting.
- Blocked people can't ask you anything.
- "Make out ++" fades to black: the game only says the lights went out.

## How it goes
1. Both open to dating, both single: **Ask on a date** from their card (with a line: where to?).
2. A yes makes you a couple (one partner at a time). Both feel better (peace of mind up).
3. Together: Hug, Kiss, Make out, Make out ++ from their card, each asked and answered. Each yes
   lifts both your minds (4, 7, 10, 14).
4. The gossip column mentions new couples and break-ups only if you both allow it.
